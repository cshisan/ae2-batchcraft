# AE2 BatchCraft

Distributes processing jobs from one pattern provider to multiple machines. Use outputs for ordinary machines, or combine material delivery, product collection and redstone control with a unit manager.<br />&#160;<br />

**Getting started:** Choose a route in [Getting Started](getting_started.md), then follow each item's page to configure it.<br />&#160;<br />

**Settings:** [Common Settings](configuration.md) covers blocking, product returns, extraction and resets. The [Unit Port Manager](unit/manager.md) page explains the four feeding modes.<br />&#160;<br />

**Other tools:** The [Fake Crafting Converter](fake_crafting_converter.md) represents jobs with tickets. The [ME Component Placer](component_placer.md) places cables and parts in bulk. The [ME IO Port](io_port.md) fills, empties and transfers storage cells.<br />&#160;<br />

**Find an item's page:** Hover over a mod item and hold the guide key (`G` by default) to open its page.<br />&#160;<br />
